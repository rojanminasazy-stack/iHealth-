How it works

Three steps, no waiting room

Built like the ride apps you already use, with the safeguards healthcare needs.

  1. Tell us what's going on

    Describe your symptoms and confirm where you are right now. Physicians can only treat you in states where they're licensed.

  2. Choose your physician

    See verified physicians who can see you today, the languages they speak, and their price.

  3. Meet by video

    Your physician starts the visit and you join from the app. Afterward you get a written summary and receipt.

Safety and privacy

Every physician is checked before they see you

Our team confirms each license with the state medical board and checks the physician's NPI before they can go online.

  • Licenses verified with state medical boards, and rechecked before they expire
  • Two-step sign-in for every physician
  • Your health information is encrypted and never sold or used for ads
  • Texts and emails never mention your symptoms or health details
  • Video visits are private and never recorded

Pricing

The price you see is the price you pay

Physicians set their own visit price, between $25 and $300. iHealthé adds one flat $9 booking fee. No membership, no surprise bills.

When is my card charged?

We place a hold when you book. You're charged only after your visit is complete. If you cancel before the physician accepts, or nobody accepts, the hold is released.

Do you take insurance?

Not yet. Many patients use an HSA or FSA card. We can give you a receipt for reimbursement.

Which states are available?

We're launching in California first. You'll only see physicians licensed in the state you're in at the time of the visit.

Is iHealthé for emergencies?

No. If you think you're having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
