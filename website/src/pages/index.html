How it works

From "I don't feel well" to a plan in about 20 minutes

As simple as the ride apps you already use, with the safeguards healthcare needs.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your email and mobile number. We confirm both, so only you can get into your account.

  2. Tell us about you

    Share why you're visiting, your health history, current medications and any chronic conditions. Your physician reads this before you meet.

  3. Meet your physician

    We match you with a verified physician licensed in your state. You talk face to face in a private 20-minute video visit and get a written summary afterward.

Why iHealthé

Our credentialing team confirms each license with the state medical board and verifies the physician's NPI before they ever see a patient. Licenses are rechecked before they expire.

Questions

Good to know

What can iHealthé physicians help with?

Everyday problems like colds, flu, sore throat, allergies, rashes, urinary symptoms, minor injuries, prescription questions and follow-ups. Your physician will tell you if you need to be seen in person.

How long is a visit?

Up to 20 minutes with your physician. Because you answer the intake questions first, your physician already knows why you're there.

When is my card charged?

For pay-per-visit, we place a hold when you book and charge you only after the visit. Memberships are billed monthly, and you can cancel any time.

Do you take insurance?

Not yet. Many patients use an HSA or FSA card, and your receipt works for reimbursement.

Which states are available?

We're opening in California first. You'll only see physicians licensed in the state you're in at the time of your visit.

Is iHealthé for emergencies?

No. If you think you're having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
