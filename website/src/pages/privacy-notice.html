Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

This notice is given on behalf of the independent physicians who see patients through iHealthé. iHealthé LLC handles your information for them as their business associate.

How your information may be used and shared

  • Treatment: your physician uses your intake and health details to care for you.
  • Payment: to charge for your visit. Payment processors receive only what's needed to charge your card, never health details.
  • Health care operations: to run, secure and improve the service, check quality, and train staff.
  • As required by law: for public health reporting, abuse or neglect reports, health oversight, court orders, law enforcement in limited cases, and to prevent a serious threat to health or safety.

We will not sell your information or use it for marketing. Any other use needs your written permission, which you can take back at any time.

Your rights

  • See and get a copy of your health records, on paper or electronically.
  • Ask us to correct your records.
  • Ask us to contact you in a certain way or at a certain address.
  • Ask us to limit what we use or share. If you pay in full yourself, you can ask us not to share that visit with your health plan.
  • Get a list of who we've shared your information with, for up to 6 years.
  • Get a paper copy of this notice at any time.
  • Choose someone to act for you.
  • Be told if there's a breach that affects your information.

To use any of these rights, email privacy@ihealthe.net. We reply within 30 days.

California

California law (the Confidentiality of Medical Information Act) may give you more protection than HIPAA. Where it does, we follow it.

Complaints

If you think your privacy rights were violated, email privacy@ihealthe.net, or file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at hhs.gov/ocr/complaints. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

Changes to this notice

We can change this notice, and the change will apply to all information we have about you. The current notice is always on this page and in the app.
