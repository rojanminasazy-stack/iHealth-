Getting started

From application to first patient

  1. Apply in the app

    Download iHealthé Pro. Enter your NPI, your state licenses and your price, and upload your CV.

  2. We verify you

    Our credentialing team checks each license with the state medical board. Most reviews take 1–3 business days.

  3. Go online

    Sign the BAA, connect payouts and start your plan. Then flip the switch whenever you want patients.
