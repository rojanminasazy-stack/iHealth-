iHealthé is a marketplace

iHealthé connects patients with independent physicians. iHealthé does not practice medicine, employ physicians, or direct their medical decisions. Each physician is responsible for the care they provide.

Not for emergencies

Do not use iHealthé for medical emergencies. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Your location

You must tell us the state you are physically in at the time of your visit. Physicians can only see you in states where they are licensed.

Payments

Patients pay the physician's visit price plus a flat iHealthé booking fee. Your card is held when you book and charged after your visit. Physicians pay iHealthé a flat monthly subscription. iHealthé takes no percentage of physician fees.
