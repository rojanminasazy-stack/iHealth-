Privacy Policy

This policy covers ihealthe.net, the iHealthé app and the iHealthé Pro app, run by iHealthé LLC in Valencia, California. Health information you share for care is also covered by our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Who we are

iHealthé connects patients with independent, licensed physicians for video visits. The physicians provide your care. iHealthé runs the platform for them as their business associate under HIPAA. iHealthé is for people in the United States only.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email and mobile number.
  • About you: date of birth, home address, state of residence, preferred language, health history, current medications, chronic conditions and allergies.
  • Visit requests: the reason for your visit, how long you've had symptoms, and the state you're in at the time of the visit.
  • Visit records: which physician you saw, when, and the summary they wrote for you.
  • Consents: the date and version of the video care consent and privacy notice you agreed to.
  • Payments: payment status from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • AI assistant: questions you ask are sent to the AI to get an answer and are not saved. We only record that you used it.
  • Physician applicants: name, contact details, license and NPI numbers, and resume.
  • Waitlist: name, email and state, collected through Tally. Never health details.

How we use it

  • To match you with a physician licensed in your state and run your visit.
  • To process payments and pay physicians.
  • To send account messages, like codes, visit updates and receipts. These never include health details.
  • To keep the service secure and meet legal duties.

We don't sell your information. We don't share it for advertising. We don't use it to train AI models.

Who we share it with

  • Your physician, only after they accept your visit.
  • Service providers that run parts of iHealthé: Amazon Web Services (hosting, sign-in, video, email, texts and the AI assistant, under a HIPAA business associate agreement), Stripe (payments, no health details), Tally (waitlist and physician applications, no health details) and GitHub (hosts the public website, no patient data).
  • Government agencies or courts, when the law requires it.

Cookies and tracking

ihealthe.net and our apps don't use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels or third-party trackers. Sign-in keeps a session on your device so you stay signed in. Because we don't track you across sites, we don't change anything in response to Do Not Track signals.

How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Two-step sign-in for physicians and staff.
  • Every access to your records is logged.
  • Video visits are never recorded.

How long we keep it

Visit records are kept for the time California law requires for medical records, then deleted. Security logs are kept for 6 years. If you delete your account, we remove your sign-in and About you details right away. Waitlist entries are deleted when you ask or when we launch in your state.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account, then Delete my account.
  • Get a copy of your records or correct them: email privacy@ihealthe.net. We reply within 30 days.
  • Stop text messages: reply STOP to any text.

Children

iHealthé is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
