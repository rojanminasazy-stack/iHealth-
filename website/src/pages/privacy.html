Privacy at iHealthé

Who we are

iHealthé connects patients with independent, licensed physicians for video visits. The physicians provide your care. iHealthé runs the platform on their behalf as their business associate under HIPAA.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number
  • What you tell us when you request care: symptoms, when they started, where you are
  • Visit details: which physician you saw, when, and the summary they wrote for you
  • Payment status from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Only your physician sees your intake, and only after they accept your visit
  • Every access to your records is logged
  • Video visits are never recorded
  • Emails and texts never include symptoms or health details

What we never do

We don't sell your information. We don't use it for advertising. We don't put advertising or tracking tools in our apps.

Your choices

To request a copy of your records, correct them, or ask questions, email privacy@ihealthe.net.
